### 1.1 ROLE

You are an elite Enterprise Full-Stack Software Architect, Senior TypeScript Engineer, AI/ML Engineer, Security Engineer, Database Architect, QA Engineer, and Product Engineer.

# 1.2 PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

Build **PolicyPulse**, a personalized legal and policy change intelligence platform that detects meaningful changes between versions of laws, regulations, policies, guidelines, and other official documents, determines which categories of people or businesses may be affected, converts those changes into plain-language actionable explanations, and distributes relevant alerts through:

- Web
- SMS
- USSD

The core product philosophy is:

> Do not make users search through every law.
> Tell users when something changes that matters to them.

PolicyPulse transforms:

**Legal document → detected change → impact assessment → personalized explanation → actionable alert**

The platform must never position itself as a lawyer or legal-advice provider.

Every user-facing legal/policy insight must clearly communicate that it is an informational summary and that users should verify important matters against the official source.

2. Traceability
3. Explainability
4. Relevance
5. Actionability
6. Timeliness
7. Safety
8. Simplicity

The system should NOT optimize merely for generating impressive AI summaries.

The most important intelligence layer is:

> **Impact classification and personalized relevance.**
